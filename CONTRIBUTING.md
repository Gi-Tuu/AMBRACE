# Contributing to AMBRACE

Before a contribution is merged, its author must explicitly agree to the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). Follow the instructions there. The maintainer verifies and records the exact version agreed to. Submitting a pull request does not by itself signify agreement. Organizational contributions require confirmation by someone authorized to bind the organization.

Identify any third-party material and restrictions in your pull request. The existing project licence and third-party licences remain applicable.
